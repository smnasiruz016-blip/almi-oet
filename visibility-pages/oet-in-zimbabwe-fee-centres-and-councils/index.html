Questions and answers

Last checked: 2026-11-06

How much does OET cost in Zimbabwe?

The full test costs AUD 587 (the official fee). In Zimbabwe that is about ZWG 10,835 — converted with ExchangeRate-API's rate of 2026-11-06 (1 AUD = 18.46 ZWG). It is approximate: you pay the official fee in AUD. Source: support.oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

How much does it cost to change an OET booking or ask for a re-mark?

OET's own page: moving a paper booking to another date, venue or test type costs AU$120 up to the booking closing date and AU$200 after. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: rescheduling the test on computer or at home costs AU$120 up to 7 days before the test and AU$200 after that. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: a re-mark costs AU$120 per sub-test. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

Can an Anesthesia Technician apply for the OET?

It does not appear so. OET's official page lists 12 eligible professions, and anesthesia technician is not among them. Check with AHPCZ, Zimbabwe's allied health regulator, before booking. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-29

Can an MDS graduate apply for the OET exam?

Yes, if you are a dentist. OET's official page lists Dentistry among its professions, and all listed professions can sit the computer test. Dentists register in Zimbabwe through the Medical and Dental Practitioners Council of Zimbabwe. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-29

Is there a specific OET route for paramedical or allied health staff?

For allied health staff in Zimbabwe, the regulator is the Allied Health Practitioners Council of Zimbabwe (AHPCZ). OET's own page lists 12 eligible professions, so confirm your profession is among them before booking. Source: ahpcz.co.zw — its official page · checked 2026-10-29

How long does it take to get OET results?

OET's own page: results for the test on paper come from 13 days after the test date. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: results for the test on computer come in as little as 48 hours after all sub-tests are done. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: 95% of results for the test on computer are released within 6 calendar days. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

Where can I take OET in Zimbabwe?

To find a test venue in Zimbabwe, use Find a test on OET's booking page. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

What rules are special to Zimbabwe?

Zimbabwe is not a party to the Hague Apostille Convention, so documents need full consular legalisation (not apostille): notarisation / High Court authentication, then authentication by the Zimbabwe Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Trade before onward legalisation. Source: hcch.net — its official page · checked 2026-10-26

Copies of medical or dental degrees submitted to the Medical and Dental Practitioners Council of Zimbabwe must be certified by a Notary Public. Source: mdpcz.co.zw — its official page · checked 2026-10-26

Foreign-trained medical and dental applicants for provisional registration must supply a notary-certified copy of their degree qualification and training transcript. Source: mdpcz.co.zw — its official page · checked 2026-10-29

A Zimbabwe-issued Certificate of Good Standing is requested from the Medical and Dental Practitioners Council by getting its CGS form from the council website or offices and having it signed by senior practitioners. Source: mdpcz.co.zw — its official page · checked 2026-10-29

NCZ issues a Certificate of Good Standing (CGS) through its official 'Clearance form for Certificate of Good Standing Verification'. The applicant supplies personal and registration details, a 10-year employment history, criminal-record and disciplinary disclosures, intended destination and departure date, and two references from nurse managers in good standing; a Ministry of Health clearance is part of the process. The CGS is transmitted board-to-board — sent directly to the receiving Registrar — and NCZ may notify the receiving board of any matter arising after issue. The clearance process is stated to take about 1–2 weeks; an applicable fee is payable on submission (amount not published). Source: nursescouncil.co.zw — its official page · checked 2026-10-26

Nurses and midwives trained outside Zimbabwe must apply to the Nurses Council of Zimbabwe to be registered before practising in Zimbabwe. Source: nursescouncil.co.zw — its official page · checked 2026-10-29

What is OET@home and how do I take it?

OET's own page: bookings for the test on computer at a venue and at home close 7 days before the test date (11:59 PM UTC). Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03
