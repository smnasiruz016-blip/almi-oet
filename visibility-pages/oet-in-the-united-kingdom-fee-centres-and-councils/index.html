Questions and answers

Last checked: 2026-11-04

How much does OET cost in the United Kingdom?

The full test costs AUD 587 (the official fee). In the United Kingdom that is about GBP 304 — converted with ExchangeRate-API's rate of 2026-11-06 (1 AUD = 0.5175 GBP). It is approximate: you pay the official fee in AUD. Source: support.oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

How much does it cost to change an OET booking or ask for a re-mark?

OET's own page: moving a paper booking to another date, venue or test type costs AU$120 up to the booking closing date and AU$200 after. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: rescheduling the test on computer or at home costs AU$120 up to 7 days before the test and AU$200 after that. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: a re-mark costs AU$120 per sub-test. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

Can a dental MDS graduate sit the OET exam?

OET's own page: OET is made for 12 healthcare professions: medicine, nursing, dentistry, dietetics, pharmacy, physiotherapy, podiatry, radiology, occupational therapy, veterinary science, optometry and speech pathology. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

How long does it take to get OET results?

OET's own page: results for the test on paper come from 13 days after the test date. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: results for the test on computer come in as little as 48 hours after all sub-tests are done. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: 95% of results for the test on computer are released within 6 calendar days. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

Where can I take OET in the United Kingdom?

OET can be taken at a test venue in the United Kingdom. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-26

Which council registers each profession in the United Kingdom?

Nursing and Midwifery Council registers nurses in the United Kingdom — https://www.nmc.org.uk. Source: nmc.org.uk — its official page · checked 2026-11-03

General Medical Council (GMC) registers doctors in the United Kingdom — https://gmc-uk.org/. Source: gmc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

General Dental Council registers dentists in the United Kingdom — https://www.gdc-uk.org. Source: gdc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-11-03

General Pharmaceutical Council registers pharmacists in the United Kingdom — https://www.pharmacyregulation.org. Its own site is at regulated-professions.service.gov.uk. Source: regulated-professions.service.gov.uk — its official page · checked 2026-11-03

Health and Care Professions Council registers physiotherapists in the United Kingdom — https://www.hcpc-uk.org. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

Health and Care Professions Council registers occupational therapists in the United Kingdom — https://www.hcpc-uk.org. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

Health and Care Professions Council registers radiographers in the United Kingdom — https://www.hcpc-uk.org. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

Health and Care Professions Council registers dietitians in the United Kingdom — https://www.hcpc-uk.org. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

General Optical Council registers optometrists in the United Kingdom — https://www.optical.org. Source: optical.org — its official page · checked 2026-11-03

Health and Care Professions Council registers podiatrists in the United Kingdom — https://www.hcpc-uk.org. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

Health and Care Professions Council registers speech pathologists in the United Kingdom — https://www.hcpc-uk.org. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons registers vets in the United Kingdom — https://www.rcvs.org.uk. Source: rcvs.org.uk — its official page · checked 2026-11-03

What rules are special to the United Kingdom?

In the UK, a driving licence is not accepted as primary ID. Refugee/asylum candidates may use a BRP, ARC or Home Office travel document. e-Visas are not accepted as ID (from 28 Jan 2025). Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

The GMC sends its certificate of good standing only directly to another medical regulator, so the doctor cannot have it sent to themselves. Source: gmc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-30

The HCPC will not issue its confirmation letter if someone requests it on the registrant's behalf or by email, so the registrant must download it from their own online account. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-30

The NMC does not complete forms supplied by other authorities, so a foreign verification form cannot be filled in by the NMC and the certificate must be requested through the applicant's own NMC account. Source: nmc.org.uk — its official page · checked 2026-10-27

What OET score do the councils in the United Kingdom ask?

NMC accepts OET: Listening B (350+), Reading B (350+), Writing C+ (300+), Speaking B (350+). Source: nmc.org.uk — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

GMC accepts OET Medicine (paper, computer or OET@Home): at least grade B in each of speaking, listening, reading and writing, in the same and most recent sitting. Source: gmc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

GPhC accepts Pharmacy OET on Paper or on Computer: at least B in each of reading, writing, listening and speaking at one sitting. OET@Home is not accepted (guidance dated June 2025). Source: assets.pharmacyregulation.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

HCPC accepts OET Physiotherapy at a test centre (not at home): 1400 total with no element below 300. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

HCPC accepts OET Occupational therapy at a test centre (not at home): 1400 total with no element below 300. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

HCPC accepts OET Dietary at a test centre (not at home): 1400 total with no element below 300. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

GOC does NOT accept OET. International optometrists must take IELTS Academic (overall 7.0, each section 6.0+, speaking 7.0+). Source: optical.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

HCPC accepts OET Podiatry at a test centre (not at home): 1400 total with no element below 300. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

HCPC accepts OET Radiography at a test centre (not at home): 1400 total with no element below 300. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

HCPC accepts OET Speech and Language at a test centre (not at home): 1800 total with no element below 400. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

RCVS accepts OET (veterinary version only, paper or online): overall grade B, with B or higher in three of four components and a minimum of C+ in one. Source: rcvs.org.uk — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

What Score is 350 in OET?

OET's own guide: grade A is 450 to 500, grade B is 350 to 440, grade C+ is 300 to 340, grade C is 200 to 290; grades D and E are below 200. Source: OET, Understanding your score · checked 2026-11-04

Which countries will accept OET scores for Nurses in 2026?

OET's own page says its results are accepted as proof of English language skills worldwide, and links to its directory of the organisations that recognise it. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03
