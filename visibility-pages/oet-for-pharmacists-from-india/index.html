Questions and answers

Last checked: 2026-11-06

How do pharmacists from India prove they are in good standing?

Indian pharmacists prove good standing with a Good Standing Certificate from their State Pharmacy Council, confirming they are registered, renewed and free of disciplinary action. The council usually sends it directly to the foreign pharmacy board, or in a sealed envelope, rather than to the pharmacist. Source: Elite Expertise — its own page · checked 2026-11-06

How do pharmacists from India get their documents attested?

The Ministry of External Affairs apostilles Indian educational documents such as degree and diploma certificates, not only personal documents. Source: mea.gov.in — its official page · checked 2026-10-26

Where do pharmacists from India go, and what score does each country ask?

In the United Kingdom: GPhC accepts Pharmacy OET on Paper or on Computer: at least B in each of reading, writing, listening and speaking at one sitting. OET@Home is not accepted (guidance dated June 2025). Source: assets.pharmacyregulation.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

In Australia: Pharmacy Board of Australia (via Ahpra). Accepts OET. Tests on/after 23 Apr 2026: Listening 350, Reading 360, Writing 350, Speaking 360, no overall score. Source: Ahpra, Transition arrangements policy (April 2026), Table 2 — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

In New Zealand: Pharmacy Council of New Zealand accepts OET: A or B in each of the four sub-tests (listening, reading, writing, speaking), within two years of application. Source: pharmacycouncil.org.nz — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

In Ireland: PSI accepts OET (Pharmacy): Listening 350, Reading 350, Writing 300, Speaking 350, all components B (350); same sitting; valid two years from test date; home-based tests not accepted. Source: psi.ie — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

In Singapore: Singapore Pharmacy Council names OET on its own page among the English tests it accepts — Listening 350 · Reading 350 · Writing 350 · Speaking 350. Source: spc.gov.sg — its official page · checked 2026-11-03

How much does an OET exam cost in India?

The full test costs AUD 587 (the official fee). In India that is about INR 38,653 — converted with ExchangeRate-API's rate of 2026-11-06 (1 AUD = 65.85 INR). It is approximate: you pay the official fee in AUD. Source: support.oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

How long does it take to get OET results?

OET's own page: in India and the Philippines, results for the test on paper come in as little as 5 days after the test date. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: results for the test on computer come in as little as 48 hours after all sub-tests are done. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: 95% of results for the test on computer are released within 6 calendar days. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03
