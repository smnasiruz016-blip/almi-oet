Questions and answers

Last checked: 2026-11-04

Which council in the Philippines registers nurses?

Office for nurses in the Philippines: Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), P. Paredes St. corner Nicanor Reyes (Morayta) St., Sampaloc, Manila 1008 — https://prc.gov.ph/. Whether it is the registering body is not confirmed yet. Source: prc.gov.ph — its official page · checked 2026-10-29

How do nurses from the Philippines prove they are in good standing?

A PRC Certificate of Good Standing requires a system-generated Action Sheet, a valid PIC or e-PIC, and documentary stamp tax per copy. Source: prc.gov.ph — its official page · checked 2026-10-28

A Filipino nurse obtains PRC-authenticated documents via LERIS (log in, book an appointment, pay, present the Action Sheet plus valid PIC/e-PIC and originals with a Documentary Stamp Tax per copy), and the PRC provides a 'Stateboard Verification / Validation of Registration/License' service used by professionals going abroad. The PRC page does not describe direct electronic transmission to a specific overseas board; third-party verifiers (e.g. CGFNS/TruMerit, DataFlow) are commonly used depending on the destination — confirm the exact handoff for the receiving regulator. Source: prc.gov.ph — its official page · checked 2026-10-28

For an overseas medical regulator, the relevant route is PRC STATEBOARD VERIFICATION / Validation of Registration: complete the Stateboard Verification Slip (from prc.gov.ph), pay P75.00 per copy plus stamps, submit with a copy of the valid PIC and Transcript at Window-C. PRC sends the verification DIRECTLY office-to-office to the requesting overseas authority ('Mailing is strictly an Office-to-Office transaction'). Alternatively, Certification/Authentication is applied for via the LERIS portal (released to the applicant). Source: prc.gov.ph — its official page · checked 2026-10-28

How do nurses from the Philippines get their documents attested?

For use in a country that is also an Apostille party, a DFA Apostille replaces embassy or consulate legalization of Philippine documents. Source: apostille.gov.ph — its official page · checked 2026-10-26

The Philippines abolished the DFA 'red ribbon' and acceded to the Hague Apostille Convention (in force 14 May 2019; embassies stopped red ribbons 15 June 2019). Documents are now apostilled by the DFA (regular PHP 100, ~5 working days; expedited PHP 200, ~2 working days) at appointment.apostille.gov.ph. Source: dfa.gov.ph — its official page · checked 2026-10-28

Where do nurses from the Philippines go, and what score does each country ask?

In the United Kingdom: NMC accepts OET: Listening B (350+), Reading B (350+), Writing C+ (300+), Speaking B (350+). Source: nmc.org.uk — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

In Australia: AHPRA sets one English standard for every board it registers, the board for nurses included (OET's own list names AHPRA for nurses) — Tests on/after 23 Apr 2026: Listening 350, Reading 360, Writing 350, Speaking 360, no overall score. Source: Ahpra, Transition arrangements policy (April 2026), Table 2 — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

In New Zealand: Nursing Council of New Zealand accepts OET: minimum 350 in Reading, Listening and Speaking; minimum 300 in Writing. Online computer-based tests are not accepted (OET on Computer at an approved venue, with the speaking sub-test hosted online, is accepted). Source: nursingcouncil.org.nz — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

In Ireland: NMBI accepts OET (Nursing): Writing C+ (300), the other three B (350); one result only, no combined results; results under two years old; OET at home not accepted. Source: nmbi.ie — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

How much does OET cost in the Philippines?

The full test costs AUD 587 (the official fee). In the Philippines that is about PHP 25,723 — converted with ExchangeRate-API's rate of 2026-11-06 (1 AUD = 43.82 PHP). It is approximate: you pay the official fee in AUD. Source: support.oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

How do I register for an OET review class?

Contact one of the three official OET review centres in the Philippines directly to enrol: Agentic in Iloilo City, ITTI Philippines in Cebu, or SGR Focus in Kalibo. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-27

Which OET test option should I choose exactly?

Choose by your profession and preferred format: OET offers paper, computer, and OET@Home delivery, so pick the mode that suits you and is accepted by your recognising organisation. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-27

Is OET also for nurses?

Yes, OET has a version designed for nurses, with tasks focused on nursing topics. Check the nursing registration body you are applying to for its own requirements. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

How can nurses get free OET exams?

OET's website offers free official sample tests for nursing, but the pages checked show no general free exam sittings for nurses. Free full tests are sometimes funded through sponsored programmes such as RefuAid. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-28

Is IELTS or OET better for healthcare workers?

A view many people hold, not an official rule: OET is designed for healthcare professionals and uses workplace medical scenarios, while IELTS is a general English test. The better choice depends on the registration body or employer you are applying to, so check what they accept. Source: oet.com — a view, not an official rule · checked 2026-10-28

Where can I take OET in the Philippines?

To find a test venue in the Philippines, use Find a test on OET's booking page. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

How long does it take to get OET results?

OET's own page: in India and the Philippines, results for the test on paper come in as little as 5 days after the test date. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: results for the test on computer come in as little as 48 hours after all sub-tests are done. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: 95% of results for the test on computer are released within 6 calendar days. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

Who can take the OET test?

OET's own page: OET is made for 12 healthcare professions: medicine, nursing, dentistry, dietetics, pharmacy, physiotherapy, podiatry, radiology, occupational therapy, veterinary science, optometry and speech pathology. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: for someone who is not a health professional working towards registration, OET may not be the most suitable test. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03
