Questions and answers

Last checked: 2026-11-04

Where do occupational therapists from Oman go, and what score does each country ask?

In the United Kingdom: HCPC accepts OET Occupational therapy, on computer or on paper: 1400 total with no element below 300. Source: hcpc-uk.org — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

In Australia: Occupational Therapy Board of Australia (via Ahpra). Accepts OET. Tests on/after 23 Apr 2026: Listening 350, Reading 360, Writing 350, Speaking 360, no overall score. Source: Ahpra, Transition arrangements policy (April 2026), Table 2 — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

In New Zealand: Occupational Therapy Board of New Zealand accepts OET (Occupational Therapy): minimum B in each of the four components; results may be combined within 12 months of the first test; results no older than 24 months. Source: otboard.org.nz — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

In Ireland: CORU (Occupational Therapists Registration Board) accepts OET (Occupational Therapy): B (350) in three sub-tests and C+ (300) in one. Source: coru.ie — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

How much does OET cost in Oman?

The full test costs AUD 587 (the official fee). In Oman that is about OMR 157 — converted with ExchangeRate-API's rate of 2026-11-06 (1 AUD = 0.2675 OMR). It is approximate: you pay the official fee in AUD. Source: support.oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

Where can I take OET in Oman?

To find a test venue in Oman, use Find a test on OET's booking page. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

How long does it take to get OET results?

OET's own page: results for the test on computer come in as little as 48 hours after all sub-tests are done. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: 95% of results for the test on computer are released within 6 calendar days. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03
