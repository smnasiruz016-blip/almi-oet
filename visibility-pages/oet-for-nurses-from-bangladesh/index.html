Questions and answers

Last checked: 2026-11-04

Which council in Bangladesh registers nurses?

Bangladesh Nursing and Midwifery Council (BNMC) registers nurses in Bangladesh — https://bnmc.gov.bd/. Source: bnmc.gov.bd — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

How do nurses from Bangladesh prove they are in good standing?

A nurse applying for the BNMC Certificate of Good Professional Standing must submit a character certificate from a registered nurse that gives that nurse's registration number. Source: bnmc.portal.gov.bd — its official page · checked 2026-10-29

How do nurses from Bangladesh get their documents attested?

Documents originating in Bangladesh must first be attested by the Consular Section of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Dhaka before the Bangladesh Embassy in The Hague will attest them. Source: hague.mofa.gov.bd — its official page · checked 2026-10-26

Bangladeshi-origin documents such as certificates must first be attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Dhaka before they are used abroad, as stated by the Bangladesh Embassy in Doha. Source: doha.mofa.gov.bd — its official page · checked 2026-10-29

Where do nurses from Bangladesh go, and what score does each country ask?

In the United Kingdom: NMC accepts OET: Listening B (350+), Reading B (350+), Writing C+ (300+), Speaking B (350+). Source: nmc.org.uk — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

In Australia: AHPRA sets one English standard for every board it registers, the board for nurses included (OET's own list names AHPRA for nurses) — Tests on/after 23 Apr 2026: Listening 350, Reading 360, Writing 350, Speaking 360, no overall score. Source: Ahpra, Transition arrangements policy (April 2026), Table 2 — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

In New Zealand: Nursing Council of New Zealand accepts OET: minimum 350 in Reading, Listening and Speaking; minimum 300 in Writing. Online computer-based tests are not accepted (OET on Computer at an approved venue, with the speaking sub-test hosted online, is accepted). Source: nursingcouncil.org.nz — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

In Ireland: NMBI accepts OET (Nursing): Writing C+ (300), the other three B (350); one result only, no combined results; results under two years old; OET at home not accepted. Source: nmbi.ie — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

How much does OET cost in Bangladesh?

The full test costs AUD 587 (the official fee). In Bangladesh that is about BDT 50,925 — converted with ExchangeRate-API's rate of 2026-11-06 (1 AUD = 86.75 BDT). It is approximate: you pay the official fee in AUD. Source: support.oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

Is there a free OET exam for nurses?

No free OET exam is listed, but OET offers free sample tests on its website for nurses to practise before paying for the real test. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-29

Where can I get study material and guidelines for healthcare professionals?

OET's own website provides study material for candidates, including sample tests, role cards and speaking guides organised by profession. Start from its Ready section for profession-specific materials. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-28

Is OET only for health professionals?

Yes, OET is designed for healthcare professionals and tailored to 12 healthcare professions, including nursing, medicine and other specialist healthcare professions. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-29

Is OET also for nurses?

Yes, OET has a version designed for nurses, with tasks focused on nursing topics. Check the nursing registration body you are applying to for its own requirements. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

Is IELTS or OET better for healthcare workers?

A view many people hold, not an official rule: Neither is better for everyone. OET usually suits healthcare workers who only need professional registration, because its content is clinical. IELTS may suit those who also need university admission or a migration pathway that requires it. Source: learninsync.in — a view, not an official rule · checked 2026-10-28

Where can I take OET in Bangladesh?

OET can be taken at a test venue in Bangladesh. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-26

How long does it take to get OET results?

OET's own page: results for the test on paper come from 13 days after the test date. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: results for the test on computer come in as little as 48 hours after all sub-tests are done. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: 95% of results for the test on computer are released within 6 calendar days. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

Can a Bangladeshi nurse use OET to study or work abroad?

OET's own page: OET tests the English of healthcare professionals who want to register and practise where English is spoken. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03
