Questions and answers

Last checked: 2026-11-10

What OET score does Dubai Healthcare City Authority ask of radiographers?

Dubai Healthcare City Authority accepts OET for radiography, among other professions, with a minimum score of C in all components. Source: Accordemy — its own page · checked 2026-11-06

Where can I take OET in the United Arab Emirates?

To find a test venue in the United Arab Emirates, use Find a test on OET's booking page. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-10

How long does it take to get OET results?

OET's own page: results for the test on computer come in as little as 48 hours after all sub-tests are done. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-10

OET's own page: 95% of results for the test on computer are released within 6 calendar days. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-10
