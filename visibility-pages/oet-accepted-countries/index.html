Where can I find new OET listening practice materials?

New OET listening practice can be found on the Benchmark OET login portal, which has more than 30 listening practice tests. The page also points to its guides for listening samples with answers and explanations. Source: Benchmark OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-07

Where can I get OET reading practice materials?

Benchmark (edubenchmark.com) offers OET reading practice for nurses, including OET Reading Sample Practice Tests, reading tips and materials, and vocabulary tests. Its OET course also includes practice tests, mock tests, eBooks and video lessons. Source: Benchmark — its official page · checked 2026-11-09
