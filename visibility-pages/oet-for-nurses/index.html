OET for Nurses

Last checked 2026-11-04

4 countries' councils accept OET for nurses on their own pages: the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland. The score each one asks is below, with its official page and the day it was checked.

Source: nmc.org.uk — its official page — read 2026-10-31

CountryCouncilWhat it asks
United KingdomNMCNMC accepts OET: Listening B (350+), Reading B (350+), Writing C+ (300+), Speaking B (350+).
AustraliaAHPRAL 350 · R 360 · W 350 · S 360
New ZealandNursing Council of New ZealandNursing Council of New Zealand accepts OET: minimum 350 in Reading, Listening and Speaking; minimum 300 in Writing. Online computer-based tests are not accepted (OET on Computer at an approved venue, with the speaking sub-test hosted online, is accepted).
IrelandNMBINMBI accepts OET (Nursing): Writing C+ (300), the other three B (350); one result only, no combined results; results under two years old; OET at home not accepted.

Questions and answers

Last checked: 2026-11-04

What OET score does NMC in the United Kingdom ask of nurses?

In the United Kingdom: NMC accepts OET: Listening B (350+), Reading B (350+), Writing C+ (300+), Speaking B (350+). Source: nmc.org.uk — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

What OET score does AHPRA in Australia ask of nurses?

In Australia: AHPRA sets one English standard for every board it registers, the board for nurses included (OET's own list names AHPRA for nurses) — Tests on/after 23 Apr 2026: Listening 350, Reading 360, Writing 350, Speaking 360, no overall score. Source: Ahpra, Transition arrangements policy (April 2026), Table 2 — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

What OET score does Nursing Council of New Zealand in New Zealand ask of nurses?

In New Zealand: Nursing Council of New Zealand accepts OET: minimum 350 in Reading, Listening and Speaking; minimum 300 in Writing. Online computer-based tests are not accepted (OET on Computer at an approved venue, with the speaking sub-test hosted online, is accepted). Source: nursingcouncil.org.nz — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

What OET score does NMBI in Ireland ask of nurses?

In Ireland: NMBI accepts OET (Nursing): Writing C+ (300), the other three B (350); one result only, no combined results; results under two years old; OET at home not accepted. Source: nmbi.ie — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

What OET score does a state board in the United States ask of nurses?

In the United States — Alabama (state board): Alabama Board of Nursing names OET on its own page among the English tests it accepts; the page does not say which score is for which sub-test — see its page. Source: abn.alabama.gov — its official page · checked 2026-11-03

What OET score does a provincial college in Canada ask of nurses?

In Canada — Northwest Territories (provincial college): College and Association of Nurses of the Northwest Territories and Nunavut (CANNN) names OET on its own page among the English tests it accepts. The page does not say which score is for which sub-test — see its page. Source: cannn.ca — its official page · checked 2026-11-03

What OET score does Allied Health Professions Council Singapore in Singapore ask of nurses?

Allied Health Professions Council Singapore's official page, checked 2026-11-04, does not say this. Source: Allied Health Professions Council Singapore — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

What OET score does Department of Health - Abu Dhabi in the United Arab Emirates ask of nurses?

OET's official page, checked 2026-11-03, does not say this. Ask Department of Health - Abu Dhabi directly. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-11-03

What OET score does The Council for Nurses and Midwives Malta in Malta ask of nurses?

OET's official page, checked 2026-11-03, does not say this. Ask The Council for Nurses and Midwives Malta directly. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-11-03

What is the OET Writing letter for nurses?

OET's own page: the Writing task's word range is 180–200 words (no automatic penalty for writing over or under it). Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: after the reading time there are 40 minutes to write the letter, and the case notes can be consulted throughout. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

Is OET accepted in the UK for an MSc in Nursing?

OET is recognised widely as proof of English, but acceptance for a specific UK MSc Nursing depends on that university's own English requirements, so confirm with the programme. Check which OET grades and sub-tests it requires. Source: occupationalenglishtest.org — its official page · checked 2026-11-02

Is OET also for nurses?

Yes, OET has a version designed for nurses, with tasks focused on nursing topics. Check the nursing registration body you are applying to for its own requirements. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

How can nurses get free OET exams?

OET's website offers free official sample tests for nursing, but the pages checked show no general free exam sittings for nurses. Free full tests are sometimes funded through sponsored programmes such as RefuAid. Source: oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-28

How much does OET cost?

AUD 587 for the full OET (four sub-tests). Source: support.oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-10-31

When do OET results come?

OET's own page: results for the test on computer come in as little as 48 hours after all sub-tests are done. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: results for the test on paper come from 13 days after the test date, in all regions but India and the Philippines. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

When do OET bookings close?

OET's own page: bookings for the test on paper close 24 days before the test date. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

Who can take the OET test?

OET's own page: OET is made for 12 healthcare professions: medicine, nursing, dentistry, dietetics, pharmacy, physiotherapy, podiatry, radiology, occupational therapy, veterinary science, optometry and speech pathology. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: for someone who is not a health professional working towards registration, OET may not be the most suitable test. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

What is OET Exam for Nurses?

For nurses, OET describes the letter as a referral letter or a discharge letter based on case notes. Source: almioet.almiworld.com · checked 2026-11-04

In OET Listening, nurses follow health-related material, which can include consultations between patients and nurses. Source: almioet.almiworld.com · checked 2026-11-04

What Score is 350 in OET?

OET's own page: grade B is a scale score of 350. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03
