Questions and answers

Last checked: 2026-11-05

How much does OET cost in Canada?

The full test costs AUD 587 (the official fee). In Canada that is about CAD 572 — converted with ExchangeRate-API's rate of 2026-11-06 (1 AUD = 0.9743 CAD). It is approximate: you pay the official fee in AUD. Source: support.oet.com — its official page · checked 2026-11-04

How much does it cost to change an OET booking or ask for a re-mark?

OET's own page: moving a paper booking to another date, venue or test type costs AU$120 up to the booking closing date and AU$200 after. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: rescheduling the test on computer or at home costs AU$120 up to 7 days before the test and AU$200 after that. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: a re-mark costs AU$120 per sub-test. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

How long does it take to get OET results?

OET's own page: results for the test on paper come from 13 days after the test date. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: results for the test on computer come in as little as 48 hours after all sub-tests are done. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: 95% of results for the test on computer are released within 6 calendar days. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

Where can I take OET in Canada?

To find a test venue in Canada, use Find a test on OET's booking page. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

What rules are special to Canada?

Transcripts and licensing statements must reach PEBC directly from the issuing body rather than through the applicant. Source: pebc.ca — its official page · checked 2026-10-30

A registration certificate does not show whether a pharmacist is in good standing, so PEBC needs a separate licensing statement that confirms it. Source: pebc.ca — its official page · checked 2026-10-30

PEBC charges $100 to send a licensing statement or good standing certificate to a provincial regulatory authority. Source: pebc.ca — its official page · checked 2026-10-30

Can we look at the role card throughout the speech, or must we keep eye Contact with the patient?

OET's own contact page gives its support site, its e-mail and the phone number +61 3 8658 3963. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

Can an MDS graduate apply for the OET Exam in Canada?

OET's own page: OET is made for 12 healthcare professions: medicine, nursing, dentistry, dietetics, pharmacy, physiotherapy, podiatry, radiology, occupational therapy, veterinary science, optometry and speech pathology. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: for someone who is not a health professional working towards registration, OET may not be the most suitable test. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

Is OET or IELTS better for Canada?

Applicants with an entry-level speech pathology degree taught in English at a university in the UK, USA, Canada, New Zealand or Ireland do not need the OET or IELTS. Source: almioet.almiworld.com · checked 2026-11-05

How can I register for the OET exam?

Go to OET's booking page, enter your city and country, and press Find a test. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: bookings for the test on paper close 24 days before the test date. Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03

OET's own page: bookings for the test on computer at a venue and at home close 7 days before the test date (11:59 PM UTC). Source: OET — its own page · checked 2026-11-03
