What does a 350 OET score indicate?

On OET's scale, a score of 350 is grade B. Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

For Listening and Reading, which have 42 questions each, grade B typically means at least 30 marks. Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

Each OET sub-test is reported on a scale from 0 to 500. Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

How long is OET speaking?

The Speaking sub-test takes about 20 minutes: two role-plays of 5 minutes each, set in the candidate's own profession. Source: OET — Speaking sub-test · checked 2026-10-15

It is recorded, and two OET assessors mark the audio using OET's Linguistic and Clinical Communication Criteria. Source: OET — Speaking sub-test · checked 2026-10-15

What OET score do I need as a speech pathologist for Australia?

Speech Pathology Australia asks for at least two OET sub-test results in the 450 to 500 band and two in the 400 to 500 band. Source: Speech Pathology Australia — OSQCA Phase One eligibility (PDF) · checked 2026-10-16

The OET results must be no more than two years old on the date of application. Source: Speech Pathology Australia — OSQCA Phase One eligibility (PDF) · checked 2026-10-16

Applicants who are not exempt must show they have passed the OET or IELTS. Source: Speech Pathology Australia — OSQCA Phase One eligibility (PDF) · checked 2026-10-16

Applicants with an entry-level speech pathology degree taught in English at a university in the UK, USA, Canada, New Zealand or Ireland do not need the OET or IELTS. Source: Speech Pathology Australia — OSQCA Phase One eligibility (PDF) · checked 2026-10-16

Each sub-test gets an estimated score on the 0 to 500 scale with a grade (A, B, C+ or C), shown as a range. No overall score is given.

Why choose the OET Test for HCPC registration?

The UK's HCPC asks speech and language therapists for an OET score of 1800 with no element below 400. Source: HCPC — English language proficiency · checked 2026-10-15

The certificate must be no more than two years old when the application is received. Source: HCPC — English language proficiency · checked 2026-10-15

The test must be taken at an official test centre, not at home. Source: HCPC — English language proficiency · checked 2026-10-15

What OET score do speech and language therapists need for CORU in Ireland?

Ireland's CORU asks speech and language therapists for at least grade B (400) in three OET components and B (350) in one. Source: CORU — Language requirements · checked 2026-10-15

Language test certificates must be dated within two years of the application. Source: CORU — Language requirements · checked 2026-10-15

CORU accepts results from at most two sittings, the repeat taken with the same provider within six months of the first. Source: CORU — Language requirements · checked 2026-10-15

CORU accepts the online and at-home versions of the OET. Source: CORU — Language requirements · checked 2026-10-15

Why are some of the OET sub-tests profession specific?

Listening and Reading are not profession specific: they are common to all candidates. Source: OET — The OET Test · checked 2026-10-15

The Writing task is specific to the candidate's own profession. Source: OET — The OET Test · checked 2026-10-15

The Speaking role-play is specific to the candidate's own profession. Source: OET — The OET Test · checked 2026-10-15

Writing and Speaking tasks are specific to the student's profession; Listening and Reading are shared by all professions.

How is the Writing sub-test marked?

Two OET assessors mark the Writing sub-test using the official OET Writing criteria. Source: OET — Writing sub-test · checked 2026-10-15

The Writing sub-test is marked against six assessment criteria. Source: OET — Writing sub-test · checked 2026-10-15

How much does an OET exam cost?

OET lists the full four-part test at AU$587 (US$455 for the computer test at a venue and OET@Home in the USA). Source: OET help centre — How much does the OET Test cost? · checked 2026-10-15

How do I book the OET for Speech Pathology?

A test is booked on OET's own website by searching for a test near you. Source: OET — Book a test · checked 2026-10-15

Bookings close 24 days before the test date for the paper test, and 7 days before for the computer test at a venue and for OET@Home. Source: OET — Book a test · checked 2026-10-15

How long do OET results take?

Paper-test results can be made available 12 days after the test date. Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

Results for the computer test and OET@Home can be made available within 48 hours of finishing all four sub-tests. Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

For how long are OET Test results valid?

OET sets no validity period itself: each body that recognises the test decides how long a result is accepted. Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

What ID do I need on OET test day?

OET accepts a valid passport or a valid national ID. Source: OET — Test regulations · checked 2026-10-15

For the computer test and OET@Home, a national ID must be in English lettering. Source: OET — Test regulations · checked 2026-10-15

A national ID that is not in English must be shown with a certified translated copy on test day. Source: OET — Test regulations · checked 2026-10-15

The ID shown on test day must match the ID used to book. Source: OET — Test regulations · checked 2026-10-15

A national ID must be issued by the government of the country where you sit the test and show your full name, photograph and date of birth. Source: OET — Test regulations · checked 2026-10-15
