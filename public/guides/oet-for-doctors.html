What is the OET test for doctors?

Listening and Reading are not profession specific: they are common to all candidates. Source: OET — The OET Test · checked 2026-10-15

The Writing task is specific to the candidate's own profession. Source: OET — The OET Test · checked 2026-10-15

The Speaking role-play is specific to the candidate's own profession. Source: OET — The OET Test · checked 2026-10-15

What OET score do doctors need?

For an OET taken on or after 23 April 2026, Ahpra's National Boards ask for at least 350 in Listening, 360 in Reading, 350 in Writing and 360 in Speaking. Source: Ahpra — Transition arrangements for changes to minimum scores for accepted English tests, April 2026 (PDF) · checked 2026-10-16

The Medical Council of Ireland asks for a pass in OET Medicine with at least grade B in each module, in a single sitting within the last two years. Source: Medical Council of Ireland — English language requirements · checked 2026-10-15

The Medical Council of New Zealand asks doctors for at least 350 in Speaking, Listening and Reading and 300 in Writing in the OET Medicine. Source: Medical Council of New Zealand — English language policy · checked 2026-10-15

What is the minimum OET score for the UK?

The UK's General Medical Council asks for at least grade B in Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking in the OET Medicine. Source: GMC — Using your OET certificate · checked 2026-10-14

All four grades must come from the same test, in the applicant's most recent sitting. Source: GMC — Using your OET certificate · checked 2026-10-14

The GMC accepts OET on paper, OET on computer and OET@Home. Source: GMC — Using your OET certificate · checked 2026-10-15

The GMC treats OET certificates as valid for two years. Source: GMC — Using your OET certificate · checked 2026-10-14

What OET score do doctors need in the USA?

For the ECFMG Certification Pathways, applicants need at least 350 in Listening, Reading and Speaking and 300 in Writing in the OET Medicine, in one test administration. Source: ECFMG — 2027 Pathways: Occupational English Test (OET) · checked 2026-10-14

A Pathways applicant who misses the score in one sub-test must retake all four sub-tests. Source: ECFMG — 2027 Pathways: Occupational English Test (OET) · checked 2026-10-15

There is no limit on the number of times OET Medicine can be retaken. Source: ECFMG — 2027 Pathways: Occupational English Test (OET) · checked 2026-10-15

For the 2027 Pathways, the OET score must be attained on or after 1 January 2025. Source: ECFMG — 2027 Pathways: Occupational English Test (OET) · checked 2026-10-14

What is the OET fee for doctors in Pakistan in rupees?

OET lists the full four-part test at AU$587 (US$455 for the computer test at a venue or OET@Home in the USA). Source: OET help centre — How much does the OET Test cost? · checked 2026-10-15

OET fee AU$587 (≈ PKR 113,833). The rupee figure is an estimate from the RBA and SBP rates of 2026-10-14, not an OET price. Source: Reserve Bank of Australia — Exchange Rates · 1 AUD = 0.7002 USD, 2026-10-14 Source: State Bank of Pakistan — USD/PKR rates · 1 USD = 276.9545 PKR, 2026-10-14

Booking one, two or three sub-tests again on paper at a venue costs AU$315, AU$415 or AU$515, each including a AU$7 booking fee. Source: OET help centre — How much does the OET Test cost? · checked 2026-10-15

How does the OET Writing sub-test work for doctors?

The Writing sub-test takes 45 minutes and asks for a formal letter on a matter of the candidate's own profession, written from case notes. Source: OET — Writing sub-test · checked 2026-10-15

How long is the OET Speaking sub-test for doctors?

The Speaking sub-test takes about 20 minutes and is made of two role-plays of 5 minutes each, set in the candidate's own profession. Source: OET — Speaking sub-test · checked 2026-10-15

What does an OET score of 350 mean for doctors?

Each OET sub-test is reported on a scale from 0 to 500 and given a letter grade from A (highest) to E (lowest). Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

Also asked: What score is 350 in OET?

How long do OET results take for doctors?

Computer-test and OET@Home results can be made available within 48 hours of finishing all four sub-tests. Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

Paper-test results can be made available 12 days after the test date. Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

How long is an OET result valid for doctors?

OET sets no validity period of its own: the bodies that recognise OET decide how long a result stays valid. Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

How do doctors book the OET?

OET's booking page covers the paper test, the computer test at a test venue and OET@Home. Source: OET — Book a test · checked 2026-10-15

Bookings close 24 days before the test date for the paper test and 7 days before for the computer test and OET@Home. Source: OET — Book a test · checked 2026-10-15

Where can doctors take the OET in Pakistan?

OET's website names Islamabad, Karachi and Lahore as the cities in Pakistan where computer-test candidates can take the Speaking sub-test at a venue. Source: OET — Speaking sub-test available at a venue for select countries · checked 2026-10-15

What ID do doctors need on OET test day?

On test day you can show your passport or national ID, and it must match the ID you booked with. Source: OET — Test regulations · checked 2026-10-15

Can doctors move their OET test to another date?

A computer-test or OET@Home booking can be moved up to two days before the first sub-test. Source: OET help centre — Can I defer/reschedule my OET Test to a different date? · checked 2026-10-15

For the computer test and OET@Home, moving costs AU$120 before bookings close and AU$200 after. Source: OET help centre — Can I defer/reschedule my OET Test to a different date? · checked 2026-10-15
