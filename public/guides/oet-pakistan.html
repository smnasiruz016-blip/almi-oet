Who uses the OET Test as proof of English proficiency?

The UK's Nursing and Midwifery Council asks for at least grade B (350) in Listening, Reading and Speaking and at least C+ (300) in Writing. Source: Nursing and Midwifery Council (UK) — Accepted English language tests · checked 2026-10-15

Two sittings can be combined if they are within 12 months of each other and all four parts were taken each time. Source: Nursing and Midwifery Council (UK) — Accepted English language tests · checked 2026-10-15

The test must be no more than two years old. Source: Nursing and Midwifery Council (UK) — Accepted English language tests · checked 2026-10-15

For an OET taken on or after 23 April 2026, Ahpra's National Boards ask for at least 350 in Listening, 360 in Reading, 350 in Writing and 360 in Speaking. Source: Ahpra — Transition arrangements for changes to minimum scores for accepted English tests, April 2026 (PDF) · checked 2026-10-16

An English test taken on or before 22 April 2026 must meet Ahpra's old minimum scores in Table 1. Source: Ahpra — Transition arrangements for changes to minimum scores for accepted English tests, April 2026 (PDF) · checked 2026-10-16

Table 1 asks for at least B in OET Listening, Reading and Speaking and C+ in Writing. Source: Ahpra — Transition arrangements for changes to minimum scores for accepted English tests, April 2026 (PDF) · checked 2026-10-16

Ireland's NMBI asks for grade B in three components and C+ in one, in a result less than two years old. Source: NMBI — English language requirements · checked 2026-10-15

It does not accept combined results or OET at home. Source: NMBI — English language requirements · checked 2026-10-15

The Nursing Council of New Zealand asks for at least 350 in Reading, Listening and Speaking and 300 in Writing. Source: Nursing Council of New Zealand — English language requirement · checked 2026-10-15

These must be reached within 12 months of the first sitting. Source: Nursing Council of New Zealand — English language requirement · checked 2026-10-15

It does not accept online tests taken outside an approved test centre. Source: Nursing Council of New Zealand — English language requirement · checked 2026-10-15

Can I defer/reschedule my OET Test to a different date? How much does it cost?

Moving a booking to another date costs AU$120 before bookings close for that test date and AU$200 after. Source: OET help centre — Can I defer/reschedule my OET Test to a different date? · checked 2026-10-15

A paper test can be moved up to 23:59 on the day before the test. Source: OET help centre — Can I defer/reschedule my OET Test to a different date? · checked 2026-10-15

A computer test at a venue or OET@Home can be moved up to two days before the first sub-test. Source: OET help centre — Can I defer/reschedule my OET Test to a different date? · checked 2026-10-15

OET ka result kitne din mein aata hai?

Paper-test results can be made available 12 days after the test date. Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

Results for the computer test and OET@Home can be made available within 48 hours of finishing all four sub-tests. Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

OET ka result kitne saal tak valid rehta hai?

OET sets no validity period itself: each body that recognises the test decides how long a result is accepted. Source: OET — Results and scoring · checked 2026-10-15

Agar aik sub-test mein score kam aaye to kya sirf wohi dobara de sakti hoon?

After a first full test, OET lets a candidate book one, two or three sub-tests again, on the paper test only and not in the USA: AU$315, AU$415 and AU$515. Source: OET help centre — How much does the OET Test cost? · checked 2026-10-15

The UK's Nursing and Midwifery Council combines two sittings only when all four parts were taken each time, so a single sub-test taken alone does not count for it. Source: Nursing and Midwifery Council (UK) — Accepted English language tests · checked 2026-10-15

Test day par passport zaroori hai ya CNIC chal jaye ga?

OET accepts a valid passport or a valid national ID. Source: OET — Test regulations · checked 2026-10-15

For the computer test and OET@Home, a national ID must be in English lettering. Source: OET — Test regulations · checked 2026-10-15

A national ID that is not in English must be shown with a certified translated copy on test day. Source: OET — Test regulations · checked 2026-10-15

The ID shown on test day must match the ID used to book. Source: OET — Test regulations · checked 2026-10-15

A national ID must be issued by the government of the country where you sit the test and show your full name, photograph and date of birth. Source: OET — Test regulations · checked 2026-10-15

OET says every OET profession, nursing included, can sit OET@Home. Source: OET — OET@Home · checked 2026-10-15

OET@Home is taken online on the candidate's own computer, with full remote proctoring. Source: OET — OET@Home · checked 2026-10-15

The candidate needs a quiet space that meets OET's technical and environmental requirements. Source: OET — OET@Home · checked 2026-10-15

OET says OET@Home may not be accepted by every recognising organisation, so candidates should check with theirs first. Source: OET — OET@Home · checked 2026-10-15

The OET@Home page shows a fee of AUD 587. Source: OET — OET@Home · checked 2026-10-15

Results can come as early as 48 hours after all sub-tests are taken. Source: OET — OET@Home · checked 2026-10-15
