## Verdicts recorded (measured, not argued)

**1. The origin × destination GRID is dead.** Same-origin cross-destination overlap 54–60%, over the 40% gate on 10 of 10 pairs. `pakistan→UK` and `pakistan→Australia` share the whole origin half. Australia's 10 corridor pages were dropped; `/register/au-ahpra` kept at 1,243 words — that is what Australia earned. Full write-up: `AlmiOET_corridor_grid_VERDICT_2026-08-06.md`.

**Root cause, reusable:** factoring a shared fact out of N *differently-worded* instances into ONE identical sentence repeated N times reduces duplicated facts and **increases** 5-gram overlap. Law #3's "de-emphasise the shared" means **link it** — wording it identically everywhere is the opposite.

**2. Medicine corridors — tested, NOT built.** Doctor vs nurse for the same country is genuinely distinct (12–26%, 0/6 over gate) — **the profession axis is real**. But the six medicine corridors overlap *each other* (35/38/48%, 6/15 over) because the doctor origins lack the per-origin FAQ and fees/timeline that make the nursing corridors distinct. Re-run `scripts/medicine-corridor-test.ts`; the verdict is at the top of the file. Revisit only if doctor origins reach nursing depth.

**3. The HCPC allied-health cluster — resolved.** Six UK professions share one regulator. Composed normally they carried ~400 identical words each: 15/15 pairs over the gate, worst 50%. Fixed by dropping and **linking** the regulator-wide sections and keeping only what differs per profession (OET version, pathway, threshold — speech and language therapy needs a higher OET, fees, search wording). **15/15 → 1/15 over gate**, worst 50% → 40%. They live at `/{profession}/uk-hcpc`; the shared HCPC process stays on `/register/uk-hcpc`.

**4. Excluded because the regulator does not accept OET** — verified against each regulator's own guidance, not blogs:

| cell | why |
|---|---|
| dentistry / UK (GDC) | GDC's English-language-controls guidance names IELTS only |

| physiotherapy / US (FSBPT) | Licensure guide names TOEFL only. FCCPT accepts OET for 8 jurisdictions — a real route, but not "FSBPT accepts OET" |
| physiotherapy / Canada (CAPR) | Policy 2.2 lists CELPIP/IELTS/PTE/TEF/TCF. No OET |

> **Singapore was NOT assessed.** It appears only as a country name inside Australia's physiotherapy FLYR pathway list. No Singapore regulator has been checked for OET acceptance — do not record it as an exclusion.

## Gates live

| gate | what it enforces |
|---|---|
| quality gate | uniqueWords ≥ 350 · facts ≥ 5 · siblingOverlap ≤ 40%, in `build` so it blocks |
| currency gate, refined | `reconfirm-official` = **indexable with a permanent caveat**, not noindex. noindex only for a load-bearing fact on a weak source, staleness (6 months; 3 for fees), or a real conflict. Applies to corridors **and** register pages |
| G6 redirect-map | the precomputed emitted list matches what the gate emits |
| **G7 index-state** | per-route indexability snapshot; fails the build on uncommitted drift, naming what moved. **G6 is structurally blind to this** — `emitted.generated.json` unions indexed and noindexed, so a page moving between them changes it by zero bytes |
