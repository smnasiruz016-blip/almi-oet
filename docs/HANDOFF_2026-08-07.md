## 1. The nationality-first test is LIVE

15 pages at `/nurse/{nationality}/uk` — nigerian, indian, filipino, pakistani, ghanaian, kenyan, zimbabwean, nepali, egyptian, sri-lankan, bangladeshi, zambian, ugandan, south-african, cameroonian. All 200.

Plus a hub at **`/nurse`** linking to all 15, and sibling cross-links (4 out, 4 in per page, zero orphans) so nothing is an orphan — the defect that left 99.7% of the old surface unindexed.

Submitted to GSC via a dedicated sitemap: **`https://almioet.almiworld.com/sitemap-nationality-nurse.xml`** — deliberately NOT listed inside `sitemap-index.xml`, so it gets its own coverage row and the batch can be tracked in isolation.

**Why this page type.** Every other page here is organised around an institution — a regulator, a country, a corridor. These are organised around the QUERY ("Nigerian nurse UK"), which is how the competitors that currently rank are built. Measured, they are the best page type on the surface by a wide margin:

| | |
|---|---:|
| cleared the gate | **15 / 15** |
| uniqueWords | 1,339 · median 1,475 · 1,668 (floor 350) |
| facts | 12 each (floor 5) |
| cross-page overlap | 20% · median 22% · 25% (ceiling 40%) |

Distinct by construction, not by composition: local-currency cost, local test-centre cities, a national migration statistic, the nationality angle, and ~16 Q&A pairs per page.

### The decision this test feeds

**Awaiting ranking — measure in ~2–3 weeks.** Baseline to compare against is the 2026-07-24 coverage export: 5,905 indexed / 144,266 discovered-not-indexed / 157 crawled-not-indexed.

The question is not whether total indexed rises. It is whether **these 15 specific URLs** reach *Indexed*, and what they do in Performance for nationality queries. Because the old surface is untouched, anything they do is attributable to the page type and nothing else — that is the entire reason they shipped isolated.

- **If they rank** → scale the Page Factory mechanism to all combinations, then prune the old 240k.
- **If they do not** → the prune is still probably right (144,266 URLs are declined at the crawl queue, not on content), but the replacement surface is not proven and should not be built at scale first.

**The prune waits either way.** `oet-pseo-rebuild` holds 48 commits including a measured 2,422-page build across 11 professions — see `docs/OET_V8_COMPLETE_BUILD_RESULT.md`. Nothing from it is deployed.

### My Progress

`/account` rendered plan + email only; the sidebar button had pointed at it since launch. It now shows AlmiPrep's four sections — Recent writing, My speaking attempts, My listening tests, My reading tests — each row with its estimate, date and a link to the result, plus per-sub-test averages.

Presentation is Prep's on purpose. The queries are not: Prep keeps four attempt tables, AlmiOET keeps one (`OetAttempt`, discriminated by `subTest`), so four `findMany` calls became one.

**Two deliberate deviations, because OET is not IELTS:**
- **No "Band X.X"** — OET reports 0–500 with an A–E grade per sub-test. Rows show `350–370 · B`.
- **No "Combined avg"** — OET publishes no composite (`overallScoreSupported()` is `false`), so averaging across sub-tests would invent the one number OET refuses to give.

Verified two ways: 12/12 assertions in `scripts/my-progress-render-check.tsx` (rendered to static markup against synthetic attempts, covering all four sub-tests, badges, links, dates, the no-estimate case and the absence of any composite), and now by 4 real scored attempts in production.
